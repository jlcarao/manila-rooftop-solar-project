Method and limits read before quoting numbers

⚠ Metro Manila baseline. No source found gives Metro Manila's own electricity consumption. This dashboard derives it as Meralco's 2025 distribution sales (about 54 TWh, which also cover nearby provinces) multiplied by an assumed Metro Manila share (default 60%, my assumption). Replace it with DOE, PSA or Meralco city-level figures. The coal share (63%) is the Luzon grid in 2023 per DOE and is not specific to Metro Manila. Oil share (1%) and emission factor (0.70 kgCO₂/kWh) are also unverified placeholders.
⚠ Coverage. The dataset is treated as Metro Manila, as instructed. It holds about 296k usable buildings, which is likely well below the real building stock, so potential may be understated. Confirm what area and sample it covers.
Source: manila_rooftop_solar_clean.csv (aggregated and embedded in this file). Technical potential only. Baselines: Meralco 2025 results (BusinessWorld), Meralco 2025 sales (Tribune), DOE 2023 gross generation. Works fully offline: no external scripts or map tiles.
