The problem

Metro Manila's power rests on imported fossil fuel and rising bills

Fossil-fuel dependence

of Philippine power came from coal in 2024, with the share projected near 60% through 2027.

More than 80% of the coal burned is imported.

Middle East oil dependence

of crude oil imports come from the Middle East (figures vary by source). The 2026 conflict triggered a fuel scare and a declared energy emergency.

Yet oil is only ~1% of power generation: the exposure is in transport and industry.

64%
Meralco bill pressure

of a Meralco bill is the generation charge. Fuel costs and a weaker peso pushed it up again in 2026.

System loss (~5%) and under-recoveries, collected over three years, are passed on to customers.
